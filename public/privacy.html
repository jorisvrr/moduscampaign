What information we use

  • Your business name and published business email address
  • Your trade or sector and country
  • Public registration or licence information shown in the source where we found you
  • Emails you exchange with us, if you reply
  • If you unsubscribe: a random reference code and the time you opted out. Our unsubscribe page does not receive or store your email address.

We contact businesses at published company addresses. We do not buy personal contact lists, and we do not use tracking pixels or link tracking in our emails.

Where we got it

From publicly available business sources, such as local contractor registers in the United States and contractor certification directories in the United Kingdom. If you would like to know the specific source for your business, reply to any of our emails and we will tell you.

Why we use it

To introduce MODUS to businesses that may benefit from it, and to arrange a conversation if you are interested. Where data protection law applies (for example the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR), our lawful basis is legitimate interests: telling relevant businesses about a product related to their work, while keeping the information we hold to a minimum and making it easy to opt out.

How to stop our emails

Use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or reply "no". We stop emailing you as soon as we process your request, and in any case within 10 business days. Opting out is free.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We use these service providers to operate our outreach:

  • Zoho (email sending and storage)
  • Vercel (hosting of this website and the unsubscribe page)
  • Upstash (storage of unsubscribe records)

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards in their data processing terms.

How long we keep it

If you do not become a customer, we delete your business details within 12 months of our last email to you. If you opt out, we keep a minimal record that you opted out for as long as we do outreach, so that we never contact you again.

Your rights

Where data protection law applies, you can ask us to access, correct or delete information about you, to restrict its use, and you can object to direct marketing at any time. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
